ReadListen Support

Read your own books aloud with a natural voice that runs right on your phone.

Need help or found a problem?
Email wind@windthorndevelopment.com. Say which phone you use and what happened, and we will get back to you.

Adding books

In your library, under Add something, tap Add to library to pick an EPUB, PDF or text file, or paste any text into the box first. You can also open a book from the Files app or another app's Share menu and choose ReadListen. With an empty library, Try a sample book adds a short one to listen to straight away.

Listening

Tap any paragraph to start reading from there. Playback continues with the screen locked, and you can pause or skip from the lock screen. Choose a different voice, and the reading speed, at the top of the reader.

Preparing a book ahead of time

Making speech uses the phone's processor. To save battery while listening, you can prepare a chapter or a whole book in advance; long books finish fastest while the phone is charging.

Problem reports

If you turn on Send problem reports in your library, the app sends a technical report when something goes wrong, with your book text removed. See the privacy policy for details.
