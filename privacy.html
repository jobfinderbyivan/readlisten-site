Privacy Policy

ReadListen for iPhone and Android · Effective 15 November 2026

ReadListen reads your own books aloud with a voice that runs on your phone. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. This page explains the few cases where anything leaves your phone.

Your books stay on your phone

Books, reading positions, bookmarks, your writing and the audio ReadListen makes are stored on your phone (and in your own device backups, if you use them), not on any server. The voice runs on the phone itself: text is turned into speech on the device, and nothing you read is sent anywhere to do it.

Pairing with your own computer (optional)

If you pair the phone with a computer of your own, the text to be read and your reading positions are sent to that computer only, on your local network, or directly to that computer over the internet if you have set it up to be reached away from home. The connection is encrypted and goes to no one else. If you never pair, nothing is sent.

Problem reports (optional)

ReadListen asks before sending any problem report. Only if you choose to send them, a report goes to the developer through the Sentry error-reporting service when the app crashes or stops working. Reports contain technical details (the app and system version, the device model, what went wrong and a short technical log) with book text removed. They carry no name, account or device identifier. ReadListen does not put your IP address in reports, and the reporting service is set not to store it.

If a problem happened on one paragraph, the app shows you that paragraph and sends it only if you tap to send it. You can turn reports off at any time with the switch in your library. Reports are kept for up to 90 days and used only to fix problems.

Reports carry no name or device ID, so we cannot pick out which ones came from you. Every report is deleted automatically after 90 days. Sentry, the service that stores them, is bound by its own terms to protect that data.

Updates on Android (GitHub version only)

The Android version downloaded from GitHub checks GitHub for a newer version about once a day. That is an ordinary web request to GitHub, which sees your IP address as any website would; nothing about you or your books is sent. The App Store and Play Store versions are updated by the store instead.

No tracking, no selling

No ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts. ReadListen does not sell or share any data. It does not collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
